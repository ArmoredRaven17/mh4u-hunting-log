## Game data and icons

The quest list, weapon names and labels in [docs/data.js](docs/data.js), and the monster, quest-type
and weapon-class icons in [docs/assets/MonsterIcons/](docs/assets/MonsterIcons/) and
[docs/assets/WeaponIcons/](docs/assets/WeaponIcons/), are generated by
[scripts/build_data.py](scripts/build_data.py) from a copy of the game extracted by the author from a
personally owned cartridge. **No game files, archives or keys are redistributed**; only the generated
data and icons are committed.

The quest list was cross-checked against [Kiranico](https://kiranico.com/en/mh4u)'s MH4U database.
No Kiranico data is included.
